# Exit Strategy

> **Purpose.** Not a plan to sell. A record of the decisions that keep every good outcome
> available — independence, acquisition, or public markets — and the ones that would quietly
> foreclose them.
>
> **Default assumption.** We are building to remain independent for ten years. Everything
> below exists so that this stays a choice rather than a constraint.
