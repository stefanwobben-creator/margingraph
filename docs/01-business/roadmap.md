# Roadmap

> **Purpose.** Sequencing and dependencies over a 10-year arc. Near-term is dated; long-term
> is described by conditions, not dates.
>
> **Rule.** A roadmap item states what becomes *possible* afterwards. If nothing is unlocked,
> it is a task, not a milestone.

## 1. Horizons

| Horizon | Window | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 0–12 months | Does anyone need this? |
| H2 | 1–3 years | Does it compound? |
| H3 | 3–10 years | Is it infrastructure? |
