It widens the range; it does not prevent a valuation. Personal costs run through the business, inconsistent categorisation and management figures that do not reconcile to the statutory accounts are normal in owner-managed companies, and every experienced buyer expects them.

## What it costs

The pattern is consistent: messy accounts do not lower the number directly. They shift the burden of proof onto you, and anything you cannot prove is assumed against you.

## What to fix, and in what order

Reconciliation first — management figures to statutory accounts, for all three years. Then separate personal costs out entirely and stop adding new ones. Then normalise consistently across the three years rather than only the most recent.

<Warning title="Do it early, not late">
  A business tidied in the final quarter is visible as tidied, and it raises the
  question of what else changed. Six months is the minimum for this to read as
  how the business is run rather than how it was presented.
</Warning>
